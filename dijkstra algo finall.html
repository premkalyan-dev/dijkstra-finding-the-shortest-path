🗺️ Dijkstra — Guided Visualizer (Glowing Tiles)

Beginner-friendly: icons, hover preview, disabled controls and step-by-step guidance.

Use a top-down map or a simple maze image. Dark areas will become walls by default.
  1. Upload an image → grid builds automatically.
  2. Click 🚩 Place START, then click a free tile on the canvas to place it.
  3. Click 🎯 Place END, then click a free tile to place it.
  4. Press Run to watch Dijkstra explore and reveal the shortest path.
